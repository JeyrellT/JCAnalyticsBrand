Commerce and orders · Restaurant · online ordering · In production

La Burrada Street Food

From menu to kitchen without losing an order.

The problem
A weekend burrito restaurant selling through WhatsApp needed to take orders without mandatory accounts, charge by SINPE without checking receipts by hand and let the kitchen see every order instantly.
What we built
An online menu where customers build their order on their phone and pay by SINPE: the system matches each payment to its order and, when in doubt, a person reviews it. The kitchen sees every order instantly on a tablet, stock is tracked by recipe and projects how much to prep from Friday to Sunday, there is a points program for regulars and the owner sees sales on a panel.

01What can JC Analytics build for my business?

Websites with bookings or orders, custom systems to organize your operation, Power BI dashboards, automation of repeated tasks, AI assistants and social media marketing. We also improve or connect the website and systems you already use. Tell us the problem and we will tell you where to start.

02How much does a custom website cost?

The website with bookings costs US$570, or US$770 with an AI assistant that guides your customers and gives them the booking link. It includes up to 5 sections, 10 services and 1 calendar; you supply text and images. Tax, domain, hosting, usage and monthly support are separate. There are 10 published packages, starting at US$60; anything that does not fit a package is quoted in writing.

03How long does a project take?

Every package publishes its timeline: from 3 to 5 business days for Excel and automated reports, up to 4 to 6 weeks for custom software; the website with bookings takes 2 to 4 weeks. Those timelines assume content, data and access are ready. Meanwhile you see progress every 72 hours in your client portal.

04What if something is not right after delivery?

Before we start you get in writing what the work includes, what it does not and how much it costs, and during the project you approve each deliverable in your client portal. After delivery you have 30 days of support: we fix defects within the delivered scope at no cost. After that, monthly support is optional, from US$30 a month on the packages that offer it.

05Do you work remotely and in English?

We are based in Costa Rica and can discuss your project in English or Spanish. We work remotely with businesses across Costa Rica. Tell us where your team is located so we can agree on communication and project logistics.

06What happens after I contact you?

We reply within 24 hours. The first conversation is 30 minutes and free of charge: we discuss your goal, current process and priorities, and you leave with a recommendation even if you do not hire us. Then you receive a proposal with the scope and a fixed price in writing, and only then do you decide whether we start. You can contact the team through WhatsApp or email.
