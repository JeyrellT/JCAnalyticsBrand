THE KEY IDEA

Start with what your business already does well. Then make sure people see it, consistently.

When it makes sense to hire marketing

If you already take good care of your customers but nobody is telling that story, or you post only when you find time and without a plan, this service is for you. It is designed for shops, restaurants, transport companies, workshops and people starting a business.

We publish for you, reply to messages in your brand’s voice and run campaigns on Meta (Facebook and Instagram) with weekly monitoring.

What we can do together

Each monthly plan is defined with you. It can include:

  • A monthly content calendar with designs that use your brand.
  • Photos and short videos recorded at your business, without stock images.
  • Publishing and replies to messages and comments.
  • Meta Ads campaigns with weekly monitoring.
  • A monthly report that shows what worked and what did not.

What to bring to the first conversation

Bring the name of your social accounts, what you sell and to whom, a few recent photos and the budget you can put into ads. If you have never run a campaign, we talk it through in that first call.

The community manager plan starts at US$150 per month and video editing at US$45 per video. The Meta Ads budget is agreed separately.
