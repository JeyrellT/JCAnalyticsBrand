THE KEY IDEA

Start with the action your customer needs to complete. Define the system around that action.

When a custom website makes sense

A website can explain your offer, help someone choose a service and create a clear path to contact. When bookings, orders or internal follow-up are part of that experience, the public page and the business process should be considered together.

We help teams in Costa Rica scope websites, interfaces and custom software. The starting point is your current workflow, the people using it and the decisions the system needs to support.

What we can define together

Every proposal starts with an agreed scope. A project may include some of the following, depending on its needs:

  • Content structure, mobile experience and accessible forms.
  • A custom visual direction and bilingual content when needed.
  • A backend, roles and management screens for your workflow.
  • Connections to existing systems and a clear handover.

Frequently asked questions

01How long does the website with bookings take?

Between 2 and 4 weeks once you hand over the content (text, images, services and hours). If the content is not ready, we can prepare it separately.

02What do I need to provide?

Text and images for up to 5 sections, the service list with prices and the availability for the calendar. That is enough to start.

03Does it include domain and hosting?

No. They are quoted separately or we use the ones you already have. We tell you exactly what is needed before starting.

04Can I extend it later?

Yes. The most requested additions are WhatsApp reminders, deposits through a payment gateway and memberships or packages. Each has a published price.
