THE KEY IDEA

A useful dashboard starts with a decision, a reliable source and someone responsible for acting.

From recurring reports to a shared view

When every meeting begins with reconciling spreadsheets, a dashboard can help create a consistent view. The first job is understanding where the data comes from, what each measure means and what people do with the result.

We work on Power BI dashboards, financial analysis and business reporting. Together, we define the audience, business questions and available sources before selecting charts.

What a dashboard project can include

The final scope depends on access to data and the way your team works. Common areas are:

  • Review of data sources, definitions and refresh needs.
  • A model and measures built around agreed business questions.
  • Clear views for the people who need to make decisions.
  • Permissions, validation and documentation within the agreed scope.

Start with one recurring decision

Choose a meeting or decision that repeatedly requires manual preparation. Bring sample field names, an explanation of your sources and the questions you cannot answer easily. Avoid sharing personal or confidential data in the initial inquiry.

A small, well-defined first version helps the team evaluate usefulness before adding more views. Cost and delivery depend on the sources, complexity and agreed scope; the package published on this page is the starting point.

Frequently asked questions

01Do I need to pay for Power BI?

The starter dashboard works with your files and needs no licenses. The management Power BI publishes to Microsoft’s service, and those licenses are quoted separately.

02What data do you need?

One clean source: a spreadsheet, a system with an export or a database. If the data is messy, we offer cleaning at a published price.

03Does it refresh on its own?

The base includes a documented manual refresh. Scheduled refresh is an add-on with a fixed price.

04What if I want more indicators later?

They are added in blocks of five, at a published price. We prefer to start with the ones that support one concrete decision.
